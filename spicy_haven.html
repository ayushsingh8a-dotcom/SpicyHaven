Our Story

Born from Passion,
Fired by Spice

Spicy Haven was founded in 2009 by Chef Akshita, who carried her grandmother's recipes from a small village in Tamil Nadu to the heart of the city. Her mission was simple: to prove that vegetarian food could be bold, fiery, and deeply satisfying.

Every masala is ground fresh each morning. Every dish is built on a foundation of love, tradition, and an unapologetic love for heat. Whether it's our legendary Mirchi Paneer or our 8-spice Dal Tadka, you'll taste the soul in every bite.
